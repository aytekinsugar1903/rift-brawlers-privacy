Rift Brawlers: Scrap Titans privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026. Publisher: Eyüp ŞEKER. This policy covers Rift Brawlers 1.0.0 and later versions.

Data we collect

Rift Brawlers is an offline arena brawler. It needs no account, does not connect to the internet and sends nothing to the developer. It does not ask for location, contacts, photos or the microphone.

Only the following is kept on your phone, inside the game's own folder:

  • Unlocked brawlers, stars, coins and upgrades
  • Your best wave in endless mode
  • Language, sound, music, vibration and stick-size settings

Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Ads

This version shows no ads. An ad network such as Google AdMob may be added in the future. If so, that network may process the advertising ID and basic device information to serve ads and prevent fraud. Google's ad privacy notice: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. This page will be updated before any ads are added.

Vibration

The vibration permission is only used for short feedback on hits and explosions, and can be turned off in Settings.

Children

There is no chat or online matchmaking. No personal data is collected from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text is posted at this address with a new date.
